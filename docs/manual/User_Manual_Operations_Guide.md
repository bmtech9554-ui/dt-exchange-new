# User guide

1. Sign in, complete required account activation and add a verified payout bank account.
2. Open Wallet → Deposit USDT. Copy the address or scan its QR. Use only the displayed TRC20 network. Do not send funds to sandbox or preview addresses.
3. After sending, tap “I Have Sent USDT”. Enter the amount and transaction hash. You may attach your receipt/screenshot, sender address, transaction time and a note.
4. Submission does not credit your balance. Review status and any request for more information appear in Deposit Review and notifications. Use Refresh status to retrieve the current server result.
5. When the deposit is approved and credited, sell up to your available USDT. The backend supplies the rate, fee and INR receivable. Review the bank account and quote before confirming.
6. INR payout remains pending/processing while the business handles its bank transfer. Completion appears only after authorized payment confirmation and ledger settlement.
7. Open transaction details for UTR, payment time and “View Payment Receipt”. Images open in the app; PDFs use your device’s save dialog. Receipts are private. Protect any copy you choose to save locally.

Download APKs only from the verified official DT Exchange website over HTTPS. Android may warn because installation is outside Google Play. Grant installation permission only to the browser you used; keep Play Protect enabled. Updates open the official download page and never install silently.

Home / Wallet / History / Profile remain the only bottom destinations. Verification remains in onboarding/account activation, not extra Profile menus.
