**Name:** DT Exchange

**Short description:** Sell USDT. Receive INR. Track your exchange from deposit to payout.

**Full description draft:**

DT Exchange helps eligible users sell supported USDT and receive INR in a verified bank account. Review the exchange rate, fees and expected INR before confirming your order. Follow deposit confirmation and payout status in one transaction timeline, and view your transaction history and available payout references.

Manage your payout bank accounts, notification preferences and active sessions. Get help through support tickets and the Help Center. Account verification, transaction limits and supported networks apply. Rates, fees, processing times and availability are shown in the app. Only send funds on the network displayed for your order.

This draft may be used only after the described production capabilities are accepted. Do not promise instant settlement, guaranteed rates, regulatory approval or returns.
