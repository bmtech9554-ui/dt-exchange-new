DT EXCHANGE — PRIVACY POLICY DRAFT
UNPUBLISHED — INTERNAL REVIEW ONLY — 8 October 2026

Publication is blocked until the legal operator's full name, address, applicable
privacy/grievance contact and official contact email are confirmed. DT Exchange
is used below only as a brand, not as a registered legal entity. No effective date
or user acceptance is implied by this draft.

Scope and contact
This draft describes the DT Exchange website at https://dtexchange.app and its
Android app. The temporary contact supplied for review is
dtexchangeservice@gmail.com. Telegram support is @dtexchange. Do not send an OTP,
password, authenticator secret, wallet seed phrase or private key to support.

Information used
Account information includes the name, mobile number, email, numeric public user
ID and authentication records needed to create and secure an account. Passwords
are stored as hashes. Authentication uses short-lived challenges and sessions.
Bank information includes the account holder, account number, bank, IFSC and any
branch or account type supplied. Transaction records include submitted amounts,
quote/rate snapshots, public receiving addresses, transaction hashes, deposit
proofs, manual review outcomes, payout references and receipts. Support includes
ticket messages and attachments where supported. Referrals include the referral
code, attribution, eligibility status and reward/release history.

Security and operational records include session/device identifiers, request
metadata, security events, administrator actions and notification preferences.
The app stores session credentials in platform secure storage. Optional biometric
unlock is performed by the operating system; DT Exchange does not receive or
store fingerprint images, face images or biometric templates. Theme preferences
are stored on the device. Session cookies used by the admin panel are secure and
HttpOnly. These descriptions do not promise that any system is risk-free.

Purposes
Information is used to administer accounts, authenticate access, provide the
requested manual transaction workflow, prevent duplicate or fraudulent requests,
support users, maintain accurate financial records and investigate incidents.
Referral information is used to attribute eligible registrations and hold rewards
separately pending manual release. Optional notification preferences can be
changed in the app; essential security and transaction messages may remain needed.

Access and sharing
Authorized staff access information only within their permitted duties. Private
proofs require authenticated user or authorized administrator access. Hosting,
SMS/OTP and other enabled service providers process information necessary for
their service. Bank/payment recipients may receive details necessary to process
an explicitly reviewed payout. Relevant records may be disclosed where required
by applicable law or valid legal process. Public blockchain addresses and
transaction hashes may already be publicly observable independently of DT
Exchange. Opening Telegram, email or a sharing app is subject to that service's
own privacy practices. A referral link includes an invite code; reward history
may identify a referred account by its numeric public ID, not its password or OTP.

Choices and requests
Users can update supported profile fields, notification preferences and device
settings, and request account closure through the app. Contact support to request
access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent where applicable, or to raise
a privacy concern. Identity may need to be checked before acting on a request.
Information essential to provide a requested service cannot simply be omitted
while continuing that service. Closure deactivates access; it does not silently
erase settled transactions, audit records, outstanding obligations or records
that must be retained by law. Applicable rights and statutory remedies remain.

Retention, security and location
The implementation uses access controls, encryption for sensitive fields and
private files, encrypted backups, authentication controls and audit trails.
Publication must include an approved retention schedule and confirmed processing
locations/provider arrangements; these have not been invented in this draft.
Retain data only as needed for disclosed purposes, legal obligations and disputes,
then apply the approved deletion/anonymization process. No indefinite blanket
retention claim is intended. Security incidents must be assessed and handled in
accordance with applicable notification and response obligations.

Changes
Publish a dated version only after approval and explain material changes before
they take effect where required. This unpublished draft must not be presented as
an effective policy, proof of regulatory compliance or a substitute for a named
operator's reviewed notice.
